# ECommerce-Delivery-and-Seller-Performance-Analytics
📖 Project Overview
---------------------

Analyzed an E-Commerce dataset to uncover insights on revenue growth, customer experience, logistics performance, and seller reliability.
Designed for executive decision-making and operational optimization using interactive dashboards with drill-through pages, toggle visuals, and seller performance segmentation.

🎯 Business Objectives
-------------------------

Monitor revenue trends & top-performing categories

Identify operational inefficiencies & SLA breaches

Measure delivery impact on customer ratings

Detect high-risk sellers driving delays

Optimize freight costs across categories & distances

🗂️ Dashboard Highlights
-------------------------
**Executive Page**

KPIs: Total Revenue, Orders, AOV, On-Time %, Average Rating

Visuals & Insights:

Revenue & Orders Trend → Reveals seasonal growth & capacity stress

Revenue by Category (Heatmap) → Shows high-revenue categories

Revenue by Payment Type → Identifies dominant transaction channels

Orders by State → Highlights regional demand concentration

Operations & Logistics

KPIs: Avg Delay Days, Freight %, Avg Delivery Days, On-Time %, % Shipping Delays

Visuals & Insights:

On-Time vs Late Deliveries → Seasonal SLA patterns & spikes

Shipping vs Logistics vs Both Delays → Identifies root causes

Freight Cost Scatter & Decomposition → Distance & seller/category cost drivers

Seller Performance Quadrant → Stars, At Risk, Underperformers, Niche Players

Pareto Analysis → 50% SLA breaches caused by few sellers

Drill-Through Pages

Category Drill-Through: Dynamic revenue/orders/AOV trend, SLA breakdown, rating vs delay → Identifies risky high-value categories

Seller Drill-Through: Top categories per seller, monthly SLA & delivery trends → Pinpoints unreliable sellers affecting business performance

📊 Key Insights

Revenue Growth: Driven by a small set of categories and regions.

Delivery Performance: Delays strongly impact customer ratings; on-time % dips during high-volume periods.

Seller Risk: Minority of sellers responsible for majority of SLA breaches. Stars set benchmarks; At Risk sellers need targeted intervention.

Freight Cost: Distance and category influence shipping cost; high-distance categories have optimization potential.

Customer Experience: Timely delivery directly correlates with satisfaction; delayed orders consistently lower ratings.

🧠 Strategic Takeaways

Focus on high-revenue sellers with poor SLA for operational improvement.

Optimize high-distance freight and category-specific shipping strategies.

Monitor top-performing categories to maintain revenue growth while improving delivery reliability.

Use quadrant segmentation to guide seller management and SLA enforcement.
