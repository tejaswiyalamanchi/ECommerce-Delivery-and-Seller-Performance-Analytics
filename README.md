# ECommerce-Delivery-and-Seller-Performance-Analytics
## 📖 Project Overview

Analyzed an E-Commerce dataset to uncover insights on revenue growth, customer experience, logistics performance, and seller reliability.
Designed for executive decision-making and operational optimization using interactive dashboards with drill-through pages, toggle visuals, and seller performance segmentation.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

Monitor revenue trends & top-performing categories

Identify operational inefficiencies & SLA breaches

Measure delivery impact on customer ratings

Detect high-risk sellers driving delays

Optimize freight costs across categories & distances

## 🗂️ Dashboard Highlights
### ***1. Executive Page***

<ins>KPIs:</ins> Total Revenue, Orders, AOV, On-Time %, Average Rating

<ins>Visuals & Insights:</ins>

  * Revenue & Orders Trend → Reveals growth patterns, demand trends, and seasonal fluctuations

  * Revenue by Category (Heatmap) → Shows high-revenue categories

  * Revenue by Payment Type → Identifies customer payment preferences

  * Orders by State → Highlights regional demand concentration

### *2. Operations & Logistics*

<ins>KPIs:</ins> Avg Delay Days, Freight %, Avg Delivery Days, On-Time %, Shipping Delay %

<ins>Visuals & Insights:</ins>

  * On-Time vs Late Deliveries → Assess delivery reliability and logistics performance 

  * Shipping vs Logistics vs Both Delays → Identifies root causes for delays

  * Freight Cost Scatter & Decomposition → Breaks down freight costs by distance, seller, and category to identify key cost drivers and patterns

  * Seller Performance Quadrant → Divides the Sellers based on their On TIme delivery% and Average Delivery Delay Days

  * Pareto Analysis → Shows the sellers that caused 50% SLA breaches

### *3. Category Drill-Through*
<ins>KPIs:</ins> Total Revenue, Orders, Frieght %, On-Time %, Average Rating

<ins>Visuals & Insights:</ins>
  * Dynamic revenue/orders/AOV trend → Tracks revenue, orders, and average order value (AOV) over time to reveal sales trends
    
  * Top sellers per category → Top 10 Sellers Driving Category Revenue
    
  * On-Time vs Late Deliveries → Assess delivery reliability and logistics performance
    
  * Rating vs delay → Highlights how delays impact customer satisfaction

### *4. Seller Drill-Through*
<ins>KPIs:</ins> Total Revenue, Orders, Frieght %, On-Time %, Shipping Delay %

<ins>Visuals & Insights:</ins>
  * Dynamic revenue/orders/AOV trend → Tracks revenue, orders, and average order value (AOV) over time to reveal sales trends
  
  * Top categories per seller → Top 10 Categories Driving Seller's Revenue
  
  *  Monthly delivery trends → Assess delivery reliability and logistics performance
  
  *  Seller Shipping SLA Compliance % by Month → highlighting individual reliability and performance

## 🧠 Strategic Takeaways

Focus on high-revenue sellers with poor SLA for operational improvement.

Optimize high-distance freight and category-specific shipping strategies.

Monitor top-performing categories to maintain revenue growth while improving delivery reliability.

Use quadrant segmentation to guide seller management and SLA enforcement.
